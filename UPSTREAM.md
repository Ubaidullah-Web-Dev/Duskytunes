# Upstream and licensing

Duskytunes is a modified GPL-3.0-or-later work derived from the open-source project originally published by SimoHypers as LiMusic. Duskytunes has its own product identity and UI, while retaining and adapting parts of the original Rust/Tauri/YouTube Music playback stack.

The original project is available at:

- https://github.com/SimoHypers/limusic

Duskytunes incorporates the functional upstream changes published after upstream v0.5.7 through v0.5.10: watch-history fallback/cache tracking, WebKitGTK dialog centering, Discord artist-status behavior, collaborative playlist handling, virtual-list scroll anchoring, artwork colour extraction, wheel volume control, settings-structure cleanup, and authenticated private-upload playback. Visual and theme changes are adapted to Dusky rather than copied wholesale.
